About EcomStrait

EcomStrait began in a two-bench workshop with a simple idea: fine jewellery should be worn, not stored in a safe. Every piece is cut, set and polished by hand from solid 18k gold and conflict-free stones, then checked against a 40-point quality list before it ships.

We buy our metals from recycled sources and our diamonds from certified suppliers, so you can trace exactly where a stone came from. Each order arrives with a certificate of authenticity and a lifetime care plan.
