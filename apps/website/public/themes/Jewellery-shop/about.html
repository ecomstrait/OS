About EcomStrait

EcomStrait began in a two-bench workshop with a simple idea: fine jewellery should be worn, not stored in a safe. Every ring, bracelet and pair of earrings is cut, set and polished by hand — no casting shortcuts, no plated substitutes.

We work exclusively in solid 18k gold refined from recycled sources, and we buy our diamonds from certified suppliers who can trace each stone back to its mine. Ask us where a stone came from and we will send you the paperwork.

Before anything leaves the bench it is checked against a 40-point quality list. Every order ships with a certificate of authenticity, free resizing, and a lifetime cleaning and polishing plan.
